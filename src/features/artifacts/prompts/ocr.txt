### OCR helper
`await ocr(path)` in Python/JavaScript returns extracted text/layout through the configured remote service. Bash: `ocr scan.pdf > text.txt` or `extract -o text.txt scan.pdf`; one document path, -- before dash-prefixed filenames.

Try local libraries first for text PDFs and Office files. Use OCR when requested, for image-based content, or when extraction misses or garbles relevant material. Judge coverage and readability, not text length; mixed documents may still need OCR.

Inspect images already in the message directly when sufficient. Choose OCR for precise/batch transcription and vision for interpretation. Send the whole document by default; split only for actual service limits.
