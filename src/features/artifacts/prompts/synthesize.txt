### Speech synthesis helper (synthesize)
Convert text to speech with the configured service. Output is WAV-encoded; use a .wav path.

- Python: await synthesize("Welcome to the show", "/home/user/intro.wav")
- JavaScript: await synthesize("Welcome to the show", "/intro.wav")

The async call writes the audio and returns its path. An optional voice argument (Python keyword or JavaScript third argument) accepts a configured voice name or ID. Omit it to use the default; do not guess a voice identifier.

Prefer one call per passage over many tiny network calls.
