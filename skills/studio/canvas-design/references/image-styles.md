# Optional image styles

Use a fragment only when that style is requested or selected. Adapt it to the subject, supplied references and explicit constraints. These choices prescribe no brand or fixed palette. Canvas reads the named bullets below for its style picker.

## Photography

- **Corporate Portrait** — an approachable professional portrait with natural proportions, soft directional light and a setting appropriate to the subject
- **B&W Studio** — a monochrome studio portrait with controlled tonal contrast, directional lighting and clear separation from the background
- **Editorial** — an editorial photograph with deliberate framing, purposeful light and space for copy only when the composition needs it
- **Stock Business** — an observational photograph of the specified work activity, with credible surroundings and natural interaction
- **Available-light Lifestyle** — a lifestyle photograph shaped by the available light, with natural color, realistic texture and deliberate depth of field
- **Warm 35mm Film** — a color-negative film treatment with gentle warmth, fine grain and soft highlight halation, preserving supplied color requirements
- **Cinematic** — a film-still composition with motivated lighting, deliberate depth and tonal grading suited to the scene
- **Macro** — close-up photography that reveals the subject's material and texture through a deliberate focal plane and controlled lighting
- **Flat Lay** — an overhead composition of the subject and relevant objects, arranged with clear hierarchy and even light

## Product

- **Product Cutout** — accurate product imagery isolated on the requested plain or transparent background, with clean edges and controlled lighting
- **Product Render** — a realistic product rendering with accurate proportions, materials, reflections and contact shadows

## Illustration and graphics

- **Flat Illustration** — simplified shapes, clear silhouettes and a coherent flat-color treatment that follows the requested palette
- **Isometric 3D** — an isometric composition with consistent projection, readable forms and controlled shading
- **Line Art** — an illustration defined by deliberate line weight, clear contours and restrained detail
- **Tech Gradient** — an abstract gradient composition with smooth tonal transitions and shapes derived from the requested subject
- **Infographic** — clear explanatory shapes and visual hierarchy, with space for exact labels and numbers to be typeset separately
- **Whiteboard Sketch** — a marker-sketch treatment with clear arrows, readable shapes and purposeful annotations
- **Blueprint** — a schematic treatment with precise linework, consistent projection and space for verified dimensions or labels
- **3D Cartoon** — stylized dimensional characters or objects with coherent proportions, expressive shapes and soft lighting
- **Sticker** — a compact, legible sticker composition with a clear silhouette and an optional cut border suited to its background

## Artistic

- **Watercolor** — translucent washes, pigment variation and visible paper texture, with edges and detail controlled around the subject
- **Sketch** — a pencil drawing with gestural lines, selective hatching and enough structure to preserve the subject
- **Art Deco** — geometric rhythm, symmetry and streamlined forms, using the requested materials and colors
- **Pop Art** — graphic simplification, bold outlines and selective halftone texture, preserving the requested color treatment

## Playful

- **Painterly Storybook** — a hand-painted narrative illustration with coherent characters, expressive setting and visible brush texture
- **Claymation** — dimensional forms with a hand-shaped clay surface, subtle imperfections and believable miniature lighting
- **Low Poly** — faceted geometry with simplified forms and consistent flat shading
